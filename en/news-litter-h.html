Litter H has arrived!

Four beautiful Pomeranian girls born on 30 May 2026 at Matej's Angels.

3 June 2026 › News › By Sanja Volner

Dear friends, I must share our happiness with you. Although we were convinced that our hearts were so full of love for Cavaliers, the little Pomeranians have definitely found their place.

At Kennel Matej's Angels FCI 103/21, on 30 May, Litter "H" arrived, four beautiful girls, three orange sable and one black & tan.

Parents:
Sire: Ch. Verfester Velikolepniy aka Tomo, orange
Dam: Ollemuar Imperial Valetta aka Maša, black & tan

I'm also sharing a photo of our Kyra's Paws Kimmy, who is Maša's daughter.

Many thanks to breeder Sandra Rajačić, Kyra's Paws pomeranian & poodle kennel, for everything!
