Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us and we'll be happy to help.

Puppies & Purchasing

Send us an email or use the contact form. Tell us a little about yourself and the dog you are looking for, and we can discuss availability and the next steps.

See our litter pages for the latest photographs and updates. For current availability or future plans, please contact us directly.

Please ask us about the documents, veterinary records and food that will accompany your puppy. We will go through the details for that litter with you before collection.

We agree the collection date with each family. Please contact us about the particular puppy you are interested in.

If you live outside Croatia, tell us where the puppy would be travelling. We can discuss whether an arrangement is possible before you make travel plans.

Health & Care

Recorded health results are listed on each dog’s profile. Please ask us if you would like to see a particular result or discuss the parents of a litter.

Ask us what your puppy is currently eating so you can prepare before bringing them home. Our care guide also includes the foods and products featured on the site.

Both breeds need regular coat care. Our care guide brings together grooming notes and products; you can also ask us about the routine for your particular dog.

About Our Kennel
