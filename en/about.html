Our story

About Matej's Angels

Cavalier King Charles Spaniels and Pomeranians from our family kennel in Premantura, Croatia.

Welcome

At home in
Premantura

Welcome to Matej's Angels. We are a small family kennel in Premantura, breeding Cavalier King Charles Spaniels and Pomeranians. Our kennel is registered under FCI number 103/21.

These pages bring together our dogs, their pedigrees and health records, our litters and photographs from life at home and at shows. We hope they help you get to know us.

Meet Our Dogs

Life with our dogs

Our dogs, our litters and the families who keep in touch.

Health records

Health information belongs to each dog. You can find the recorded results on their individual pages, alongside pedigrees and breeding assessments.

Growing up here

Our dogs are part of our family. The litter albums follow the puppies as they grow, with photographs from their early weeks and updates from their new homes.

At the shows

Shows are part of our life with dogs. We share results in the news and keep individual titles and assessments with each dog’s profile.

Keeping in touch

If you have a question about a puppy from us, please get in touch. It is always lovely to hear how our dogs are getting on.

Our litters

Each litter has its own page, with the parents, dates and photographs together in one place. Contact us to ask about a particular puppy or future plans.

How it started

Our story began with Bernese Mountain Dogs and an Irish Setter. The chronology follows the dogs and litters that brought us to Matej’s Angels today.

Our Breeds

Cavalier King Charles Spaniel

Cavaliers have been part of our family for years. We love their gentle nature and the company they give us, at home as much as in the show ring.

Meet our Cavaliers on the dogs page, where each profile brings together their photographs, pedigree and recorded results.

Pomeranian
