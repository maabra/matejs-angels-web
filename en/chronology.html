Our earlier kennel, Bernerpetrinia, was the beginning of our life in breeding.

2005.

First Bernese Mountain Dogs

Our first Bernese Mountain Dogs joined the family.

2005.

First Irish Setter

Our first Irish Setter joined us.

2006.

First Cavalier King Charles Spaniels

Cavalier King Charles Spaniels became part of our family.

Opening of Matej’s Angels Kennel

Matej’s Angels was established, continuing our work with Cavaliers and, later, Pomeranians.
