Kimmy has received her breeding approval

Kimmy passed her breeding evaluation with a “Very good” rating and received her breeding approval.

19 September 2026 › News › By Sanja Volner

We are proud to share some lovely news: on 19 September 2026, our Kimmy successfully passed her breeding evaluation and received her breeding approval.

The judges were delighted with her, and Kimmy received a “Very Good” rating. We are thrilled about this important step for our little Pomeranian.

Thank you to the judges for their assessment and kind words. We are so proud of Kimmy!
