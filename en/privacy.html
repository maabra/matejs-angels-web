This notice explains how personal data is handled when you visit matejsangels.com or contact Matej's Angels. It is intended to describe the site's current practices under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It is not legal advice.

What data is collected and why?

  • Website delivery and security: the hosting provider, Plus Hosting (plus.hr), may process technical access information such as IP address, requested page, date and time, browser/device information and referrer in server logs to deliver and secure the website and troubleshoot faults. The legal basis is the controller's legitimate interest in operating a secure, available website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Messages you choose to send: this site has no data-submitting contact form. Email and telephone links let you contact us using your own email or phone service. If you send us a message, we use the contact details and message contents to reply and handle your enquiry. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible agreement (Article 6(1)(b)) or our legitimate interest in responding to other enquiries (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Optional services: only after you opt in, the site may request the Flag Counter visitor counter for analytics, and Google Maps and Google Fonts for external content and presentation. These services can receive technical data such as your IP address and browser information. They are not loaded before consent.

We do not use this site for advertising, behavioural profiling, newsletters or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Cookies, browser storage and your choices

The site itself does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It stores your optional-service choices in your browser's local storage so it can remember them for up to 183 days. This storage is used only to remember consent choices; it is not used to track you. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using “Cookie and privacy settings” in the footer. If you withdraw consent, optional content is removed and no further requests to those services are made from the page.

Flag Counter is an optional analytics service. Google Maps and Google Fonts are optional external content. If enabled, those providers may use their own technologies and process data under their own terms. You can reject either category and still use the rest of the website. Closing the consent notice is not treated as consent.

Recipients and international transfers

Plus Hosting provides website hosting. If you opt in, technical requests are also sent to Flag Counter and/or Google (Google Maps and Google Fonts). These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Please review their current privacy information and safeguards before opting in: Google Privacy Policy and Flag Counter. We do not sell personal data.

How long is data kept?

Consent choices remain in browser storage for no more than 183 days unless you clear them sooner. Enquiry messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and manage the enquiry, and longer only where required to meet legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The hosting provider controls the retention period for its technical server logs; contact Plus Hosting for the applicable period. Optional providers apply their own retention terms when you consent to their services.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions and limits in the GDPR, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restriction of processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal. You may also lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP) at azop.hr, or with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise a right or ask a privacy question, email sanjaimatej@gmail.com or write to the postal address above. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the site's services or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
